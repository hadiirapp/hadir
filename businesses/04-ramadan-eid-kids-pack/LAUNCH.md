# Business #04: Ramadan & Eid Kids Activity Pack (printable, Arabic + English)

**Model:** a seasonal printable PDF. Ramadan 2027 starts around **February 8, 2027** and Eid al-Fitr around **March 10, 2027**, so listing it now gives Etsy and Pinterest 3–4 months to start ranking it in search before demand peaks. It sells again every year at no extra cost.
**Startup cost:** $0 (Etsy charges $0.20 per listing).
**Your time to launch:** about 40 minutes.

## What's built
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `product/Ramadan-Eid-Kids-Activity-Pack.pdf` | **The product**, 33 pages (see the list below) |
| `product/FREE-SAMPLE-Ramadan-Pack.pdf` | 3-page free sample (cover, calendar, lantern) to collect emails or post on Pinterest |
| `marketing/listing-1..5.jpg` | 5 listing images (2000×2000) made from the real pages |
| `site/index.html` | Sales page |
| `src/` | Scripts that rebuild everything (`build_pack.py`, `build_mockups.py`) |

**What's inside (33 pages):**
- Calendar: 30 days of good deeds (colour a lantern each day)
- Trackers: the 5 daily prayers × 30 days, and 30 parts of the Quran
- 5 colouring pages: lantern, mosque, crescent moon & stars, Eid gift, the musaharati drum (the Ramadan wake-up drummer)
- A "Ramadan Kareem / Eid Mubarak" colouring page with bubble letters in Arabic and English
- 2 word searches (English + Arabic), 2 mazes, and answer keys at the back
- Journal pages: "My Ramadan Journal" and "My Eid Day"
- 30 good-deed cards for a jar, plus a jar label
- Bunting: RAMADAN ★ KAREEM, plus رمضان كريم and عيد مبارك
- 4 Eid cards, 8 gift tags, and a "Ramadan Star" certificate

All the drawings are original (drawn in code). No images were copied from the internet.

## Pricing
- **$6.99** (similar Etsy listings sell for $4–$12).
- **Bundle with business #01 (Little Learners): $11.99**. "Ramadan bundle for kids" is a strong listing.
- January: a sale ("Early-bird Ramadan 30% off").

## Launch steps
1. **Etsy:** create a Digital listing with the 5 images and the PDF. Make **3 listings** aimed at different searches: "Ramadan activity pack kids", "Ramadan printables", "Eid activities kids".
2. **Gumroad / Payhip:** same files, $6.99.
3. **Pinterest:** this is where most Ramadan traffic comes from. Post 5 pins a week starting **now**, plus the free sample as a lead magnet.
4. **Facebook groups and Instagram for Muslim parents** (including ones in Europe and America): share the free sample first.
5. **Masjid Sunday schools / Islamic schools:** offer a classroom licence at $19.
