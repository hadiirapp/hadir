I can't log in

Check your venue code, username and password with your manager. Managers can reset a staff password from Team. Owners can contact us to reset theirs.

Clock in says I'm not at the venue

Allow location access for Hadir, turn on precise location, and try again inside the venue. Your manager can adjust the venue location and radius in More.

New phone

Each staff login works on one phone. Log in on the new phone and your manager approves it from the Now screen.

Delete your account

Owners: open More, tap Delete account and type the venue name to confirm. The venue and all of its data are permanently deleted within 7 days. Staff and managers: ask the venue owner to remove you, or send us a request from More, Delete account, and we delete it within 7 days.
