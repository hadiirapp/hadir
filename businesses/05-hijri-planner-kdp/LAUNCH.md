# Business #05: 2027 Hijri & Gregorian Weekly Planner (Amazon KDP paperback + printable PDF)

**Model:** two channels from one file.
1. **Amazon KDP (print-on-demand):** Amazon prints the book, ships it, and pays you royalties. No stock, no shipping, nothing up front.
2. **Etsy / Gumroad:** the same file sold as a printable PDF.

**Startup cost:** $0. Ordering a printed proof copy from Amazon (about $5 plus shipping) is optional and recommended.
**Your time to launch:** about 60 minutes.
**Timing:** planners sell most from **October to January**, and Muslim buyers look for one before Ramadan (February 8, 2027). **We are in that window now.**

## What's built
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `product/KDP-interior-2027-Hijri-Planner-8.5x11.pdf` | **The book interior:** 150 pages, 8.5×11 inches, no bleed, margins to KDP specs (0.625" inside, 0.5" outside) |
| `product/KDP-cover-2027-Hijri-Planner.pdf` | **Full wrap-around cover** (back + spine + front): 17.5878 × 11.25 inches, spine 0.3378" (150 pages × 0.002252" for white paper), 0.125" bleed, with the barcode area left clear |

**What's inside:**
- Title page and "How to use" (English + Arabic)
- 2027 at a glance: all 12 months, with the matching Hijri months under each
- Islamic dates for 2027:
  - 1 Ramadan: Mon Feb 8
  - Eid al-Fitr: Tue Mar 9
  - Day of Arafah: Sat May 15
  - Eid al-Adha: Sun May 16
  - Islamic New Year 1449: Sun Jun 6
  - Ashura: Tue Jun 15
- A goals page with 6 areas (faith, family, health, work/study, money, personal growth)
- A 30-day Ramadan planner (fasting, the 5 prayers, Taraweeh, Quran, good deeds)
- 12 monthly spreads: a calendar where each day shows both dates, the month's Islamic dates, goals, an 8-habit tracker, and notes
- 53 weekly spreads, Monday to Sunday: each day has the Gregorian and Hijri date and 5 prayer circles, Friday is highlighted as Jumu'ah, and each week has priorities and a to-do list
- Notes pages

> ⚠️ Hijri dates are **calculated** using the Umm al-Qura calendar. In some countries, moon sighting can shift a date by one day. The book says so clearly, on the "How to use" page and the Islamic dates page.

## Pricing
- **Amazon paperback: $14.99.** Amazon's printing cost for 150 black-and-white pages at 8.5×11 is roughly $2.80–3.60. The royalty is 60% of the price minus printing, so roughly **$5.40–6.20 per copy**. Check the exact figure in KDP's royalty calculator before you publish.
- **Printable PDF on Etsy: $6.99.**

## Launch steps: Amazon KDP
1. Go to kdp.amazon.com, sign in with your Amazon account, and fill in the **tax** details (W-8BEN if you're outside the US) and your bank details. This step needs your ID and bank account.
2. Create → **Paperback**:
   - Title: `2027 Hijri & Gregorian Weekly Planner`
   - Subtitle: `Islamic Planner with Prayer Tracker, Ramadan Planner, Hijri Dates 1448–1449 | Monday Start | 8.5 x 11 | English & Arabic`
   - Author/brand: Noor Planners (or any name you choose)
   - Low-content book: ✅ yes (no ISBN needed if you choose low-content, though a free KDP ISBN is fine too)
   - 7 keywords: `islamic planner 2027`, `hijri calendar 2027`, `muslim planner`, `ramadan planner`, `prayer tracker journal`, `arabic english planner`, `salah tracker`
   - Categories: Self-Help > Time Management; Religion > Islam
3. Print options: **Black & white interior, white paper, 8.5 x 11, No bleed, Matte cover**.
4. Upload `KDP-interior-…pdf` and `KDP-cover-…pdf` (choose "Upload a cover you already have").
5. Launch Previewer → check it → set the price to $14.99 → Publish. Review takes 24–72 hours.
