**Model:** a digital spreadsheet template. Budget spreadsheets are one of the biggest digital categories on Etsy, because adults pay for tools that save them time.

What the planner does:
- **Dashboard:** pick a month and you get income, expenses, net and savings rate, plus a budget-vs-actual table where each category shows ✓ or ✗. It also has a 12-month summary with a running balance and 2 charts.
- **Savings Goals:** shows % done and how much to save each month to hit each goal by its date.
- **Debt Payoff:** shows how many months until each debt is paid off, the debt-free date, and the total interest (calculated with NPER).
- **Bills:** a ✓ to tick off each bill for every month of the year.
- Checked: 1,358 formulas, 0 errors. The example numbers were calculated by hand and match.

## Launch steps

### 1. Google Sheets version (Etsy buyers expect it)
1. Upload the xlsx to your Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets: File → Save as Google Sheets.
2. Share → "Anyone with the link" → **Viewer**.
3. Copy the link and change the end from `/edit...` to `/copy`. Whoever opens that link gets a "Make a copy" button.
4. Make a one-page PDF with the `/copy` link and the Excel download link. That PDF is the file buyers download on Etsy.
